Follow Chili
== 

Follow Chili App is about the campus cat Chili, who wanders around campus.

Within the app you can spawn Chili on any detected floor. From there on you can interact with her in different ways, such as:
- Spawn a toy, Chili will follow the toy, or bring you the toy so you can throw it.
- Spawn some food, and Chili will eat the food within a few seconds.
- Call Chili, so you can pet her.

The app welcomes you with a start screen, that prompts the user to click the start button and scan the floor.
